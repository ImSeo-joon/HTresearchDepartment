The department

Research is not a sprint through a checklist. It is a chain of commitments, each one answerable to the next.

The High Table Research Department is a working atelier, not a credential mill. It exists for the long middle of a project: the weeks when a question is still soft, the evidence is incomplete, and the argument has not yet earned its confidence.
